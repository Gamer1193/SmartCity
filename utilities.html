Save water and power

Simple steps that help the whole city.

At home

  • Fix dripping taps and running toilets quickly.
  • Use a bucket instead of a hose to wash vehicles.
  • Switch off appliances at the wall when not in use.

Waste

  • Separate wet and dry waste before collection.
  • Compost kitchen waste where you can.
  • Hand e-waste to authorised collection points.
